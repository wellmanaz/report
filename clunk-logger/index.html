How to get useful data

  1. A passenger runs the phone. The driver doesn't touch it.
  2. Use an empty parking lot. Lay the phone flat and let it sit still for 3 seconds after Start (it calibrates "down" from that).
  3. Do 6–10 cycles: pull away gently, get to 10–15 mph, stop gently. Pause 3 s between cycles.
  4. If a passenger is along, they can tap HEARD IT when they hear or feel the noise. That's optional: after the run, open Review on the saved recording, play it back boosted through headphones, and tap Mark here each time you hear it.
  5. Stop, change position, repeat. Get at least driver floor, passenger floor, 2nd row, and cargo floor. Front vs rear and left vs right is what narrows it down.
  6. Faint noise? Put the phone flat on bare floor or carpet with its back touching the floor, as close as you can get to where the noise seems to come from. The motion sensor picks up a clunk through the body even when the mic barely hears it. Turn off the radio, fan and A/C.
  7. Also do one forward/reverse run: roll forward a few feet, stop, roll back, stop.
  8. Export the zip and send it to Claude.
Keep this screen open while recording. Audio is recorded with noise suppression and auto-gain turned off so short clunks aren't filtered out. GPS positions are saved as meters from the starting point, not as map coordinates.
